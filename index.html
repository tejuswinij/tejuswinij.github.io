01 / ABOUT

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity is my
common thread.

I’m Tejuswini, a final-year Food Technology student interested in food processing, quality assurance, food safety, and product development.

Through internships and laboratory work, I’ve gained practical exposure to dairy processing, HACCP, GMP and GHP, sensory evaluation, extraction techniques, and experimental design. I hope to apply that knowledge to develop safe, high-quality, value-added food products and processes.

Outside the lab, I draw portraits, scenery, and original art. Both interests begin in the same place: close observation.
